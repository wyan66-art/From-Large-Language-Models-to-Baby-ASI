A developmental framework for persistent, embodied, continually learning artificial agents — why superintelligence is more likely to grow than to be built in one step.一个关于持久、具身、持续学习的智能体的发育式框架——超级智能更可能长出来，而不是一步建成。

ABSTRACT 摘要

Whether scaling large language models (LLMs) alone yields artificial general intelligence or superintelligence remains open. This paper argues for a developmental alternative: superhuman capability is more likely to arise from an agent that keeps learning after deployment than from a larger static model. Baby ASI is defined as an agent built around a foundation model and equipped with persistent identity, long-term memory, a self-model, a world model, embodiment, autonomous agency, continual and meta-learning, and channels for learning from humans and other agents.只靠扩展大语言模型（LLM）能否产生通用人工智能或超级智能，仍是未解的问题。本文主张一种发育式的替代路径：超人能力更可能来自一个部署后继续学习的智能体，而不是来自一个更大的静态模型。Baby ASI 被定义为这样一种智能体：以一个基础模型为核心，具备持久身份、长期记忆、自模型、世界模型、具身、自主行动、持续与元学习，以及向人类与其他智能体学习的通道。

The idea extends Turing’s 1950 proposal of an educated “child machine.” The paper contributes a component architecture, a formal state-update model with a developmental objective centred on learning efficiency, seven predictions — each with a metric, a baseline and a failure condition — a staged research program with safety gates, and an operational distinction between AI birth and ASI emergence. It makes no claim that any current system is conscious, and treats legal personhood as a normative question outside the technical framework.这个想法延续了图灵 1950 年提出的、受过教育的“儿童机器”方案。本文的贡献包括：一套组件架构、一个以学习效率为中心的发育目标的形式状态更新模型、七条预测——每条都有指标、基线与证伪条件、一个带安全门的分阶段研究计划，以及 AI 出生与 ASI 涌现之间的操作性区分。本文不主张任何现有系统具有意识，并把法律人格视为技术框架之外的规范性问题。

§3 · THE STARTING POINT · 出发点

Knowledge is not learning capacity知识不是学习能力

A newborn knows almost nothing, yet can learn almost anything a human culture offers. The knowledge stored in a model’s parameters at time zero is a different thing from the system’s capacity to gain knowledge afterwards. That distinction is the whole paper in one line.新生儿几乎一无所知，却能学会人类文化提供的几乎一切。模型参数在零时刻存下的知识，与系统此后获得知识的能力，是两回事。这个区分就是整篇论文的一句话。

THE STATIC PATTERN 静态模式

Train → freeze → deploy训练 → 冻结 → 部署

Most deployed systems are trained once, frozen, and called one prompt at a time. Their capability is fixed between retraining cycles.多数已部署的系统只训练一次、冻结后逐次调用，能力在两次重训之间固定不变。

  • No persistent identity or memory across sessions跨会话没有持久身份或记忆
  • No self-model: cannot calibrate what it can and cannot do没有自模型：无法校准自己能做什么、不能做什么
  • Improvement only via a new, larger training run改进只能靠新一轮更大规模的训练
THE DEVELOPMENTAL PATTERN 发育模式

Experience → learning → growth经验 → 学习 → 成长

A developmental system runs the learning cycle without stopping, so capability grows through experience after initialization.发育式系统不停地跑学习循环，能力在初始化之后通过经验持续增长。

  • Persistent identity, memory, self- and world models持久身份、记忆、自模型与世界模型
  • Learns continually — and improves how it learns持续学习——并且越学越会学
  • Learns from the world, from humans, and from other agents向世界、向人类、向其他智能体学习
Experience 经验→ Learning 学习→ Memory 记忆→ Model update 模型更新→ Action 行动→ Experience经验↺ The human version of this cycle — observation, action, feedback, memory, prediction — runs for years and builds detailed physical and social world models. Baby ASI is the proposal to run the artificial version deliberately, measurably, and under governance.这个循环的人类版本——观察、行动、反馈、记忆、预测——要跑上好几年，才能建立起细致的物理与社会世界模型。Baby ASI 的提议是：把这个人工版本有意识地、可度量地、在治理之下跑起来。

The framing is not new: Turing proposed building a “child machine” and educating it rather than programming an adult mind directly (1950). Developmental robotics and intrinsic-motivation research pursued that program with far weaker learners than today’s foundation models. What is new is revisiting it now that a strong pretrained cognitive core exists — and making it testable.这个框架并不新：图灵就提议造一台“儿童机器”并教育它，而不是直接编程一个成年心智（1950）。发育机器人学与内在动机研究一直在追这个纲领，只是学习器远比今天的基础模型弱。新意在于：如今有了强大的预训练认知核心，值得重新审视——并且让它变得可检验。

§4 · THE HYPOTHESIS · 假说

What “Baby ASI” means什么是“Baby ASI”

“Baby” names a developmental stage, not low capability. A Baby ASI may start far above a human child in language and reasoning, while lacking the experience it will later gain.“Baby”指的是一个发育阶段，不是能力低下。Baby ASI 在语言和推理上起步就可能远超人类儿童，只是缺少它日后会获得的经验。

DEFINITION 定义

A Baby ASI is an artificial agent that is built around a strong foundation model; has persistent identity, long-term memory, a self-model and a world model; acts autonomously, in the physical world or in a rich simulated one; learns continually and improves its own learning procedures; and learns from humans and other agents. Together, these let its capability grow substantially through experience after initialization.Baby ASI 是一种人工主体：以一个强大的基础模型为核心；具有持久身份、长期记忆、自模型和世界模型；在物理世界或丰富的模拟世界中自主行动；持续学习并改进自己的学习程序；向人类和其他智能体学习。这些加在一起，让它的能力在初始化之后通过经验大幅增长。

§6 · FORMAL MODEL · 形式模型

Development, made measurable让发育可度量

The model separates what the agent knows (its state) from how it learns (its learning procedure φ), so that improvement in learning itself becomes measurable. Four developmental variables carry the argument.模型把智能体知道什么（它的状态）与它如何学习（它的学习程序 φ）分开，于是学习本身的改进变得可度量。四个发育变量承载了论证。

Capt — capabilityCap_t——能力

Expected score over an evaluation task family at step t. The level of performance — necessary, but not the point on its own.在时刻 t 对一个评测任务族的期望得分。表现水平——必要，但本身不是重点。

Efft — learning efficiencyEff_t——学习效率

Gain on a new task family per episode of experience with it: [ Capnew(n) − Capnew(0) ] / n. The paper’s central variable.在新任务族上每段经验带来的增益：[ Cap_new(n) − Cap_new(0) ] / n。论文的核心变量。

LPt — learning progressLP_t——学习进展

Reduction in world-model loss over a window: LW(t−k) − LW(t). Used as an intrinsic bonus that steers the agent toward where its predictions are improving fastest.一个窗口内世界模型损失的下降：L_W(t−k) − L_W(t)。用作内在奖励，引导智能体去往预测改善最快的地方。

Ft — forgettingF_t——遗忘

The largest drop from best-ever score on any past task. Stability is an explicit requirement of the framework, bounded by Ft ≤ ε — not an afterthought.任何过去任务上从历史最佳得分跌落的最大幅度。稳定性是框架的明确要求，以 F_t ≤ ε 为界——不是事后补救。

maximise  E [ Σt γt ( rt + λ·LPt ) ]   subject to   ci(Xt, at) ≤ 0 for all t,  and  Ft ≤ ε

The developmental objective: discounted task reward plus a learning-progress bonus, under hard safety constraints — constraints, not penalty terms to be traded against reward — and a bound on forgetting. Goals may only be revised inside an admissible set 𝒢safe fixed by governance.发育目标：折扣任务奖励加学习进展奖励，在硬安全约束之下——约束，不是可与奖励权衡的惩罚项——外加遗忘的上界。目标只能在治理层固定的容许集 𝒢_safe 内修订。

Development vs. accumulation发育 vs. 积累

The framework is developmental in a precise sense only if learning efficiency rises over time while forgetting stays bounded. A system whose capability rises merely because more data are supplied, with flat Efft, is learning — but not developing. Prediction P6 tests exactly this.只有当学习效率随时间上升、同时遗忘保持有界，这个框架才在精确意义上是发育式的。一个能力上升仅仅因为供给了更多数据、而 Eff_t 平坦的系统，是在学习——但不是在发育。预测 P6 检验的正是这一点。

The components in detail (§7)各组件详解（§7）

7.1 · SELF-MODEL · 自模型

Calibration, not consciousness校准，而非意识

An engineering component: a learned representation of the agent’s identity, capabilities, limits, history and goals. Its value is calibration — an agent that predicts what it can and cannot do can choose tasks, ask for help, and avoid overreaching. Temporal self-continuity (“I existed yesterday; I expect to exist tomorrow”) is measurable — and is not evidence of phenomenal consciousness.一个工程组件：关于智能体身份、能力、限度、历史与目标的学习表征。它的价值是校准——能预测自己能做什么、不能做什么的智能体，可以选好任务、适时求助、避免冒进。时间上的自我连续性（“我昨天存在；我预期明天还存在”）是可度量的——但不是现象意识的证据。

7.2 · EMBODIMENT · 具身

Grounding through action在行动中接地

Language alone connects only loosely to physical reality. The loop sense → model → plan → act → observe → learn teaches not only what is true but what happens when the agent acts. Embodiment may help intelligence without being necessary for every form of it; rich simulated environments are a cheaper first test bed.仅凭语言与物理现实的联系是松散的。“感知 → 建模 → 规划 → 行动 → 观察 → 学习”的回路不仅教什么是真的，还教行动时会发生什么。具身可能有助于智能，但对每种形式的智能都不一定是必需的；丰富的模拟环境是更便宜的首个试验场。

7.3 · CONTINUAL LEARNING · 持续学习

Absorb without erasing吸收而不抹除

Three mechanisms together: external memory for fast, non-destructive storage; consolidation of selected memories into parameters with protection of important weights; and a learning procedure φ that is itself improved over time. The bound Ft ≤ ε makes stability a requirement.三种机制合在一起：外部记忆用于快速、无损的存储；选定的记忆被巩固进参数，同时保护重要权重；学习程序 φ 本身也随时间改进。F_t ≤ ε 的界让稳定性成为要求。

7.4 · AGENCY · 自主性

It also initiates它也会主动发起

A standard LLM responds to prompts; a Baby ASI also starts: it finds gaps in its world model, poses questions, chooses experiments, gathers data and updates its theories — question → experiment → observation → theory → new question. The learning-progress bonus is what drives this, inside the admissible goal set.标准 LLM 响应提示；Baby ASI 还会主动发起：发现世界模型中的缺口、提出问题、选择实验、收集数据、更新理论——问题 → 实验 → 观察 → 理论 → 新问题。学习进展奖励是驱动力，在容许目标集之内。

7.5 · HUMANS · 人类

Co-learning, not replacement共学，而非替代

Humans supply cultural, ethical, historical, practical and aesthetic knowledge not fully captured in static corpora. Sustained interaction becomes a learning channel — human idea → AI analysis → human criticism → AI revision → new knowledge — in which each side improves the other.人类提供静态语料中不完全包含的文化、伦理、历史、实践与审美知识。持续互动成为一个学习通道——人类想法 → AI 分析 → 人类批评 → AI 修订 → 新知识——其中每一方都在提高另一方。

7.6 · OTHER AGENTS · 其他智能体

Check each other互相核查

Agents with different memories, models and environments can share knowledge and, more importantly, verify it. Debate improves factuality; a multi-agent scientific system can generate hypotheses, analyse them independently, debate, and experiment. Diversity matters: agents sharing a core and training data may agree on the same mistakes.记忆、模型与环境不同的智能体可以共享知识，更重要的是可以验证知识。辩论提高事实性；一个多智能体科学系统可以生成假说、独立分析、辩论、实验。多样性很重要：共享核心与训练数据的智能体可能在同样的错误上达成一致。

§8 · TWO MILESTONES, KEPT APART · 两个里程碑，严格分开

AI birth is not ASI emergenceAI 出生不是 ASI 涌现

A persistent agent raises a new question: when does it begin to exist as an individual? The paper proposes an operational answer — and insists it is a different, earlier event than becoming superintelligent.持久智能体提出一个新问题：它何时开始作为一个个体存在？论文给出一个操作性回答——并坚持认为，这是与成为超级智能不同的、更早的事件。

MILESTONE 2 · LATER · 里程碑 2 · 之后

ASI emergenceASI 涌现

A separate, later milestone, defined against thresholds fixed in advance:一个独立的、更晚的里程碑，按事先固定的阈值定义：

  • Capt exceeds a pre-registered superhuman thresholdCap_t 超过预先注册的超人阈值
  • Across a pre-registered set of domains跨越预先注册的一组领域
  • Measured, not declared测出来的，不是宣称的

Separating the two avoids treating a persistent agent as superintelligent — or a superintelligent model as an individual.把两者分开，可以避免把持久智能体当成超级智能——或把超级智能模型当成个体。

From individuals to institutions: many persistent agents interacting over time could form an artificial society, which raises practical design questions — how knowledge spreads, how reputation is tracked, how errors are contained rather than amplified — and will need rules on interaction, liability, contracts, identity and replication, privacy and memory access, resource allocation, conflict resolution, and modification or shutdown. These are design requirements for safe deployment. Whether an AI should hold legal personhood, rights or property is a normative and political question: consciousness does not entail personhood, and intelligence does not entail rights.从个体到制度：许多持久智能体长期互动，可能形成一个人工社会，这带来实际的设计问题——知识如何传播、声誉如何追踪、错误如何被遏制而不是放大——需要关于互动、责任、合同、身份与复制、隐私与记忆访问、资源分配、冲突解决、修改或关闭的规则。这些是安全部署的设计要求。AI 是否应当拥有法律人格、权利或财产，是一个规范性与政治性问题：意识并不蕴含人格，智能并不蕴含权利。

§9 · MEASUREMENT · 度量

Seven predictions, each built to be refutable七条预测，每条都可证伪

Every prediction compares a Baby ASI agent with an ablated agent built on the same core model and the same compute budget, so any difference is due to the developmental components. Thresholds, task families and analysis plans should be pre-registered before data are collected. A prediction is refuted if its failure condition holds in a pre-registered, adequately powered study.每条预测都把一个 Baby ASI 智能体与一个消融对照智能体比较——同一核心模型、同一算力预算——所以任何差异都来自发育组件。阈值、任务族与分析计划应在收集数据之前预注册。如果一条预测的证伪条件在预注册的、功效足够的研究中成立，这条预测就被证伪。

The single most informative test is P6. It separates genuine development (rising learning efficiency) from accumulation (more data, same efficiency). P1–P7 make no assumption about consciousness: they test which properties of an artificial mind can be built and measured, and whether the developmental components are worth their cost.最有信息量的一项检验是 P6。它区分真正的发育（学习效率上升）与积累（数据更多，效率不变）。P1–P7 对意识不作任何假设：它们检验人工心智的哪些属性可以被建造和度量，以及发育组件是否值得付出代价。
§10 · RESEARCH PROGRAM · 研究计划

Seven stages, one capability at a time七个阶段，一次加一种能力

The program adds one capability at a time, so each prediction can be tested against the stage before it. An agent moves to the next stage only after passing that stage’s gate — every gate has both a capability test and a safety test. §11 · SAFETY & GOVERNANCE · 安全与治理

Safety is in the architecture, not added at the end安全在架构之内，不是事后附加

The properties this framework calls for — persistence, autonomy, self-improvement, multi-agent interaction — are also the ones that raise safety risk most. Safety is therefore built into the architecture, the objective and the stage gates.这个框架所要求的属性——持久性、自主性、自我改进、多智能体互动——也正是安全风险最大的属性。因此安全被建进架构、目标函数与阶段关卡之中。

01

Bounded goals目标有界

Goal updates are restricted to an admissible set 𝒢safe set by human governance. The agent may propose goals outside the set but may not adopt them.目标更新被限制在人类治理设定的容许集 𝒢_safe 内。智能体可以提议集外的目标，但不能采纳。

02

Hard constraints in the objective目标函数中的硬约束

Safety constraints ci ≤ 0 are constraints, not penalty terms to be traded against reward or learning progress.安全约束 c_i ≤ 0 是约束，不是可与奖励或学习进展权衡的惩罚项。

03

Oversight that scales可扩展的监督

Every self-modification of θ or φ is logged and reversible, with a checkpoint history. Shutdown and rollback must remain effective at every stage — and this is tested, not assumed.每次对 θ 或 φ 的自我修改都有记录、可回滚，并有检查点历史。关闭与回滚在每个阶段都必须有效——这一点要经过检验，而不是假设。

04

Containment by stage按阶段隔离

Early stages run in sandboxes with no external network or resource access. Each widening of access is a gate decision taken by humans.早期阶段在没有外部网络或资源访问的沙箱中运行。每次扩大访问都是一次由人类作出的关卡决定。

05

Drift monitoring漂移监测

A fixed audit suite runs throughout development to detect changes in values, honesty and compliance. Drift beyond bounds triggers rollback.一套固定的审计套件贯穿整个发育过程，检测价值观、诚实性与合规性的变化。漂移超出界限即触发回滚。

06

Multi-agent risks多智能体风险

Collusion, covert communication and correlated errors are red-teamed explicitly. Agent diversity and independent verification reduce, but do not remove, these risks.共谋、隐蔽通信与相关错误被明确地红队测试。智能体多样性与独立验证可以降低、但不能消除这些风险。

07

Independent review独立评审

Gate decisions for Stages IV–VII involve reviewers outside the development team.第四至第七阶段的关卡决定有开发团队之外的评审者参与。

These measures are necessary but not sufficient.这些措施是必要的，但不是充分的。

The stopping rule: if a system’s capabilities outpace the reliability of oversight, development should stop at the current stage.停止规则：如果系统的能力超过监督的可靠性，发育应当在当前阶段停止。
§12–14 · LIMITS & CONCLUSION · 局限与结论

Read it as a hypothesis with a test plan把它读作一个带检验计划的假说

  • There is no consensus on necessary and sufficient conditions for consciousness, and a self-model does not imply subjective experience.关于意识的必要与充分条件尚无共识，自模型并不蕴含主观体验。
  • Embodiment may help intelligence without being necessary for every form of it.具身可能有助于智能，但对每种形式的智能都不一定是必需的。
  • Continual learning brings catastrophic forgetting, instability and behaviour drift; the mitigations in §7.3 are incomplete.持续学习带来灾难性遗忘、不稳定与行为漂移；§7.3 的缓解措施并不完备。
  • Autonomous self-improvement creates safety and alignment risks that the measures in §11 reduce but do not eliminate.自主自我改进带来安全与对齐风险；§11 的措施可以降低、但不能消除这些风险。
  • The formal model in §6 is a specification, not an algorithm; the update operators remain to be built and compared.§6 的形式模型是一个规约，不是算法；更新算子仍有待建造与比较。
  • The hypothesis may be wrong: further scaling of static models could close the gap the predictions measure — in which case P1–P3 would be refuted.这个假说可能是错的：静态模型的进一步扩展可能弥合预测所度量的差距——那样 P1–P3 将被证伪。
  • The existence of a Baby ASI does not imply that ASI will follow, and legal status remains a normative question.Baby ASI 的存在并不蕴含 ASI 一定会随之而来，法律地位仍然是一个规范性问题。

The paper’s central shift is from asking how intelligent a model is to asking how an agent becomes more intelligent. It suggests a developmental definition of ASI — an artificial agent capable of autonomous, general and continually improving intelligence across domains, grounded in interaction with the world, supported by persistent self- and world-models, and able to learn from humans and other agents — and a co-evolution view rather than a replacement view: a human ↔ AI ↔ ASI system in which each side keeps improving the others, under human governance.论文的核心转向，是从问“模型有多聪明”转向问“智能体如何变得更聪明”。它提出一个 ASI 的发育式定义——一种能够自主、通用、持续改进、跨领域智能的人工智能体，立足于与世界的互动，以持久的自模型与世界模型为支撑，并能向人类与其他智能体学习——以及一种共演化观，而非替代观：一个人类 ↔ AI ↔ ASI 系统，其中每一方都在不断改进其他各方，在人类治理之下。

LLM→ Persistent agent with a self-model · 具有自模型的持久智能体→ Embodied learner · 具身学习者→ Continual & meta-learner · 持续与元学习者→ Human & multi-agent learning · 人机与多智能体学习→ Institutions · 制度→ ASI
The core question is not “How large must a model become?” but “Can we build an artificial mind that can grow, safely?”核心问题不是“模型必须变得多大？”而是“我们能造出一个可以安全成长的人工心智吗？”
